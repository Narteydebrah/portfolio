Research Interest

Research Question

How can machine learning and optimisation be applied not just to understand trade and logistics systems, but to actively improve them: removing friction, predicting failure and making supply chains smarter?

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning, combinatorial optimisation and operational intelligence, applied to trade and logistics, where inefficiency is costly and data is underused. My BSc thesis on traffic signal optimisation was a first proof of concept, and my MSc dissertation applies surrogate modelling and optimisation to manufacturing process parameters, balancing quality, energy use and cost. Future directions include hybrid reinforcement learning and metaheuristic solvers for freight routing, early warning systems for supply chains, and data integration for trade operations.

Towards Real-Time DoS Detection: A Multi-Objective Optimized SVM Framework using Kernel Approximation and Dimensionality Reduction
Intrusion Detection SVM Nyström Method Bayesian Optimisation

George Debrah, Dr. Eng. Loubna Ali and Youssef Ali. Proposes a lightweight framework for real time DoS detection combining PCA, Nyström kernel approximation, a linear SVM and multi objective Bayesian optimisation. Achieved up to 99.97% recall on BoT-IoT and cut training time by over 97%, evaluated across UNSW-NB15, CIC-IDS2017 and BoT-IoT.
